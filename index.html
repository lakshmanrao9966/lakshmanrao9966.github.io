Research focus

Ph.D. thesis, Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, 2024 Study of Mechanical and Corrosion Behavior of Nimonic 263 Alloy due to Constrained Groove Pressing and Heat Treatment Supervisor: Prof. Amit Kumar Gupta

The problem

Nimonic 263 alloy, a nickel-based superalloy, having exceptional strength and corrosion resistance, is widely used for high temperature applications in aero-engine components. Despite its favorable properties, Nimonic 263 alloy often exhibits a coarse grain structure, which can limit its mechanical performance.

Particularly for sheet materials, CGP offers a promising solution to refine the grain structure and thus enhance the mechanical properties and the high-temperature performance of the alloy.

During the literature survey, no research has been reported on the CGP of the Nimonic 263 alloy, either at room or high temperature.

Equipment
Compression Testing Machine (CTM) with a capacity of 2000 kN, attached with a 3-zone split furnace
Heat treatment
700 °C, 800 °C and 900 °C; holding time 2 hours and 8 hours
Characterization
Optical Microscope (OM), Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM), Electron Backscatter Diffraction (EBSD), X-Ray Diffraction (XRD), tensile testing, micro-hardness testing
Corrosion
Hot corrosion over 100 hours at 800 °C; electro‑corrosion by EIS and PDP

Key results

  • CGP resulted in substantial improvements in YS, UTS, and micro-hardness, with increases of 234.58%, 43.79%, and 110.31% respectively compared to the as-received material.
  • The CGP process induced grain refinement, reducing the average grain size from 220.70 µm to 106.02 µm, 117.09 µm, and 94.01 µm at room temperature, 250 °C, and 450 °C respectively.
  • The CGPed specimen at 800 °C was the most effective in improving the microstructural and mechanical properties of Nimonic 263 alloy.
  • Heat-treated specimens, especially those treated at 700 °C (CGPHT700), exhibit superior corrosion resistance compared to untreated and CGP specimens.

Constrained Groove Pressing of Nimonic-263 Alloy

In this chapter, the effect of CGP on Nimonic 263 alloy was characterized through the first pass of the experimentation from room temperature to 450 °C.

Chapter 5

Heat-Treatment on CGPed Specimens: Mechanical and Microstructural Behaviour

This study investigated the effect of constrained groove pressing (CGP) followed by heat treatment (HT) on the microstructure and micro-hardness of Nimonic 263 alloy.

Chapter 6

Heat-Treatment on CGPed Specimens: Corrosion Behavior

This study explains the corrosion resistance of Nimonic 263 alloy, focusing on the effects of CGP combined with subsequent heat treatment.
